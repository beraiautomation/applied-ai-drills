# Northwind Bikes Staff Handbook

This handbook is made up for practice. Northwind Bikes is a small bike shop with two stores and an online store.

## Opening hours

The Leeds store opens at 9:00 and closes at 18:00 from Monday to Saturday. On Sundays it opens at 10:00 and closes at 16:00. The York store keeps the same hours but closes at 17:00 on weekdays in winter, from November to February. Both stores are closed on 25 and 26 December.

## Returns and refunds

Customers can return an unused bike within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. Accessories such as helmets, locks and lights can be returned within 14 days if the packaging is unopened. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days. Bikes that have been ridden can't be refunded, but they can be exchanged within 30 days for store credit.

## Repairs and servicing

A basic service costs 45 pounds and includes brake and gear adjustment, a safety check and tyre pressure. A full service costs 95 pounds and also includes cleaning the drivetrain and truing the wheels. Most repairs are finished in 3 working days. Customers get a text message when the bike is ready to collect.

## Northwind Care membership

Northwind Care costs 9 pounds a month. Members get two free basic services a year, 20 percent off parts and priority booking in the workshop. Members can cancel at any time from their online account. The membership pauses automatically if a payment fails twice.

## Electric bike batteries

E-bike batteries have a 2-year warranty. Staff must never open a battery case. A swollen, cracked or very hot battery must be put in the metal fire box in the workshop and reported to the manager the same day. Batteries should be stored between 10 and 20 degrees and charged to about 60 percent for long storage.

## Delivery

Online orders over 500 pounds get free delivery. Smaller orders cost 15 pounds to deliver. Bikes are delivered fully built and checked. Delivery takes 3 to 5 working days in the UK. We don't deliver bikes outside the UK, but accessories can be shipped to Ireland.

## Staff discount

Staff get 30 percent off bikes and 40 percent off accessories and clothing. The discount is for the staff member and one family member living at the same address. Staff can buy one discounted bike every 12 months. The discount can't be used together with sales or other offers.

## Holiday and time off

Full-time staff get 28 days of paid holiday a year, including public holidays. Holiday requests go to the store manager at least 2 weeks before. No more than two people from the same store can be off at the same time in April, May and June, because spring is the busiest season.

## Sick leave

If you are sick, call your store manager before your shift starts. A text message is not enough. For absences longer than 7 days, you need a note from a doctor. Sick pay starts from the fourth day of absence.

## Uniform and safety gear

Staff wear the green Northwind shirt and dark trousers. In the workshop, safety glasses and closed shoes are required. Gloves must be worn when handling brake fluid or degreaser. The company replaces two shirts a year for free.

## Bike fitting

A bike fitting takes 45 minutes and costs 60 pounds. The cost is taken off the price if the customer buys a bike within 30 days. Fittings are done by trained fitters only, using the fitting jig in the Leeds store.

## Cash and card payments

Both stores accept cards, phone payments and cash. Cash over 300 pounds must be counted by two people. At closing time, the till is counted and the cash goes into the safe. Card terminals must be charged overnight.

## Lost property

Items left in the store are kept in the office for 30 days. Write the date, item and where it was found in the lost property book. After 30 days, items are given to charity, except phones and wallets, which go to the police.

## Customer complaints

Listen to the customer, say sorry for the problem, and write it in the complaints log. A manager must reply within 2 working days. If a customer asks for a refund outside the rules, only a manager can approve it.
